How to play
  • Roll a 6 to bring a pawn out of your base. A 6 also gives you another roll.
  • Move clockwise around the board. Landing on a lone opponent pawn sends it back to its base.
  • Pawns on a start square or a star square are safe.
  • Three 6s in a row loses your turn.
  • You need the exact number to reach the centre. First to bring all four pawns home wins.
  • You have 10 seconds for each decision, or the game plays for you.
